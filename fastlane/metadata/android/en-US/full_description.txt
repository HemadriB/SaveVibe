SaveVibe is the fastest way to save the WhatsApp statuses you love — photos and videos, in full quality, with one tap.

★ SAVE STATUSES IN ONE TAP
Watch a status in WhatsApp, open SaveVibe and it's already there. Tap ⬇ and it's in your gallery. Works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.

★ NEVER MISS A STATUS
• New status alerts: get a gentle notification when new statuses are ready to save
• Auto-save: turn it on once and every new status lands in your gallery automatically
• Home-screen widget: see how many new statuses are waiting at a glance

★ SET UP IN 5 SECONDS
No confusing permissions. SaveVibe shows you exactly which button to tap, and asks for access to the WhatsApp status folder only — nothing else on your phone.

★ REPOST & SHARE
Repost a status to WhatsApp, share it to any app, or keep it in your SaveVibe album. Swipe through photos and play videos right in the app.

★ REELS & VIDEOS FROM LINKS
Copy a link or tap Share → SaveVibe to save your own or permitted public videos in HD.

★ LIGHT, FAST & IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Small download, works on older phones, dark mode, and available in English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், বাংলা, मराठी, Português and Bahasa Indonesia.

★ FEWER ADS
No ads until you've saved something. Share SaveVibe with a friend or watch one short video to go ad-free.

PRIVACY
SaveVibe only reads the WhatsApp status folder you allow. Your saved files stay on your phone.

Please respect the rights of content creators: only save and repost content you have permission to use.
